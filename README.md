# 📊 Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

### Turning Data into Better Business Decisions

## 📌 Project Overview

This is a self-initiated Data Analytics portfolio project focused on analyzing retail sales and profitability using **Microsoft Excel, Power Query, and Power Pivot**.

The main objective is to understand where revenue and profit come from, identify underperforming areas, investigate profitability, and evaluate business performance against targets.

The project focuses not only on dashboard creation but also on developing business understanding and generating actionable insights from data.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** — Data analysis, calculations, and dashboard creation
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation
- **Power Pivot** — Data modeling and relationship management
- **DAX** — In this Project ive calculated all the calculations by uing DAX only not excel-formulas
## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on three important business questions:

**1. Where is the money actually coming from?**
- Identify the products and categories contributing most to revenue and profit.
- Analyze performance across stores, regions, and sales channels.
- Understand the key drivers of business performance.

**2. Where are we losing money or missing opportunities?**
- Identify underperforming products, stores, and regions.
- Investigate products with high revenue but low profitability.
- Explore potential opportunities to improve profit margins.

**3. Is the business on track?**
- Compare actual performance against targets or plans where data is available.
- Monitor key financial and operational KPIs.
- Identify performance gaps that require further investigation.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The project considers relevant business metrics, depending on the available data:

- Revenue / Net Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin (%)
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Sales and Profit Contribution (%)
- Actual vs. Target Performance

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation

Power Query is used to prepare the data for reliable analysis.

The data preparation workflow includes:

- Inspecting data quality and column structures.
- Identifying and investigating duplicate records.
- Handling missing values and data inconsistencies.
- Correcting data types and standardizing formats.
- Checking for unusual values and potential outliers.
- Preparing clean tables for data modeling and analysis.

Data quality decisions are made carefully to avoid removing valid business transactions.

## 🏗️ Data Modeling

Power Pivot is used to organize the data and establish relationships between relevant tables.

The modeling process focuses on:

- Understanding the purpose of fact and dimension tables.
- Organizing data for efficient analysis.
- Establishing appropriate relationships between tables.
- Supporting consistent calculations across products, stores, regions, and channels.

## 💡 Key Insights to Investigate

The analysis is designed to uncover business insights such as:

- Which products and categories contribute most to revenue and profit?
- Which stores and regions are performing well or require attention?
- How does profitability differ across sales channels?
- Are high-revenue products also generating high profits?
- Which areas may offer opportunities to improve profitability?
- Where does actual performance differ from business targets?
